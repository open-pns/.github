## Open PNS

Open PNS is a Project aimed at developing an open platform for buiding sEMG wearables. 

### Why

OpenBCI and similar platforms have made EEG acquisition accessible to researchers and hobbyists. Purpose-built open hardware for sEMG - multi-channel, dry electrode, wrist-worn - hasn't had the same treatment. Gesture decoding, prosthetic control, and HMI research all depend on quality sEMG acquisition. Most accessible options are either single-channel, gel-dependent, or closed hardware.

### Projects
___
| Repo | Description | Status |
|------|-------------|--------|
| dial | MyoWare sEMG exploration, proof of concept | Archived |
| acquisition | Open hardware sEMG acquisition platform | Active |
| slam | Spatial mapping and pose estimation | Planned |
| interface | Full system human-facing control layer | Planned |

Signal interpretation sits somewhere between acquistion and interface. not sure where yet.

### Current Status
___
Early stage. acquisition hardware in design phase as of June 2026.
